# v0.50.0 release notes — draft, not released

**Decision:** blocked. This document is not a release announcement or support
claim. The [qualification manifest](../tests/integrations/pg-mdm/evidence/v0.50.0/manifest.json)
has no final artifact tuple, named workload, measured limits, or passed release
gates.

## Intended profile

The proposed profile is an optional pg-mdm adapter for queue routing, due-date
controls, and bounded escalation for one named, non-RLS administrative domain.
Human stewardship remains with MDM. Approval support, extra tenants, external
transports, and broader platform claims are out of scope.

## Current blockers

- The shared MDM intent contract, public projection, and packaged MDM artifact
  are not qualified; see [MDM readiness issue #5](https://github.com/trickle-labs/pg-mdm/issues/5).
- The workload owner, fixed cohort, complete shadow review, numeric limits, and
  observation record are not supplied.
- No recovery, upgrade, or clone run has qualified the final v0.50 artifact
  tuple. The prior v0.49 selected profile passed its repeated 48-check
  logical-restore audit; see the [v0.49 disposition](v0.49-qualification.md).
  That earlier result does not substitute for v0.50 final-tuple recovery.
- No v0.50 numerical limits or final operator, release-owner, and independent
  reviewer sign-offs are available.

## Publication and recovery

The merged MDM 0.15.0 candidate contract v4 adds decision-age tracking and
immutable receipt-to-publication correlation. The adapter query pack reports
`PENDING_PUBLICATION`, `PUBLISHED`, `SUPERSEDED_BEFORE_PUBLICATION`, and
`UNKNOWN_LEGACY`, plus decision age for pending stewardship. The candidate has
no published package release; real-cohort queries and final qualification
remain outstanding. This draft does not describe a released or supported
deployment.

Use a coherent whole-database physical backup with matching artifacts only
after the exact v0.50 restore drill passes. Start restored or cloned managed
effects disabled and require the documented reconciliation barrier before
resuming. This draft does not claim that physical restore has passed for v0.50.

## Limits and support

No case, churn, backlog, latency, retention, or recovery limit is published.
See the [draft support matrix](v0.50.0-support-matrix.md). The profile remains
opt-in and unsupported until every required manifest gate passes and the final
go/no-go record is signed.
