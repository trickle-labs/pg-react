# M33 known limitations

- External usability and controlled-pilot results are evidence inputs and
  cannot be generated by CI.
- v1 supports the exact tuple in [`v1-support-matrix.md`](v1-support-matrix.md);
  other PostgreSQL versions, operating systems, and architectures need their
  own qualification.
- External delivery is at-least-once. Consumers own deduplication.
- Logical restore requires the documented rebuild and reconciliation barrier.
- Simulation, replay, comparative backtesting, and why-changed analysis are
  deliberately deferred to M34 and later.
